Comprehensive Electronic Security Solutions

EXPERT SECURITY renders expert advice on electronic solutions like CCTV, access control, burglar alarms, GPS assisted warning devices, barriers, and more. Our electronic security solutions complement our manned guarding services to create a comprehensive security ecosystem.

CCTV Surveillance Systems

State-of-the-art closed-circuit television systems for real-time monitoring and recording. Our CCTV solutions include:

  • High-definition cameras with night vision capabilities
  • Remote access and cloud storage options
  • Real-time monitoring by trained security guards
  • Motion detection and alert systems
  • Professional installation and maintenance

Access Control Systems

Advanced access control solutions including biometric systems, card readers, keypad entry, and automated gate systems. These systems work in conjunction with our security guards to manage and monitor access to your premises effectively.

Burglar Alarms & Intrusion Detection

Comprehensive alarm systems including motion detectors, door/window sensors, glass break detectors, and panic buttons. Our security guards are trained to respond immediately to alarm activations, ensuring rapid response to potential threats.

GPS Assisted Warning Devices

Advanced GPS tracking and warning systems for vehicles, assets, and personnel. These devices enable real-time location tracking and emergency alert capabilities, enhancing the effectiveness of our security guard operations.

Barriers & Physical Security Equipment

Installation and management of security barriers, bollards, turnstiles, and other physical security equipment. Our security guards operate and monitor these systems to control access and prevent unauthorized entry.

24/7 Monitoring

Continuous monitoring of all electronic security systems by trained security personnel ensuring immediate response to any security breach.

Professional Installation

Expert installation and configuration of all electronic security systems by certified technicians ensuring optimal performance.

Maintenance & Support

Regular maintenance, updates, and technical support to ensure your electronic security systems operate at peak efficiency.

Integration Services

Seamless integration of electronic security systems with manned guarding services for comprehensive protection.
