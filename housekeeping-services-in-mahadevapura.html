Residential Housekeeping Services

Maintaining a clean and hygienic home environment is critical for health, comfort, and daily living. Expert Housekeeping Services provides comprehensive residential housekeeping solutions for independent houses and villas, apartments and gated communities, and serviced residences.

Our residential housekeeping services include:

  • Routine Cleaning and Dusting: Floor sweeping, mopping, and polishing; surface dusting of furniture, fixtures, and décor; cleaning of walls, switchboards, and reachable ledges
  • Kitchen and Pantry Cleaning: Countertops and cabinet exteriors; appliance surfaces (microwave, oven, refrigerator); sink and drain area cleaning
  • Bathroom and Toilet Sanitation: Deep cleaning of fittings, tiles, and floors; disinfection of high-touch surfaces; removal of stains and mineral deposits
  • Vacuuming and Carpet Care: Vacuum cleaning of carpets, rugs, and upholstery; spot cleaning and deodorizing
  • Window and Glass Cleaning: Interior glass surface cleaning; frame and sill dust removal
  • Waste Management and Disposal: Segregation of dry and wet waste; proper disposal as per local guidelines

Our residential housekeeping enhances the comfort and hygiene of your living space, creating a cleaner, healthier home environment.

  • Floor and Surface Care: Mopping, scrubbing, and polishing; spill cleanup and stain removal
