Our residential housekeeping services include:

  • Routine Cleaning & Dusting: Floor sweeping, mopping, and polishing; surface dusting of furniture and décor; removal of cobwebs and reachable dust
  • Kitchen & Pantry Cleaning: Cleaning countertops and cabinet surfaces; degreasing stove tops and appliances; sink sanitation and drain maintenance
  • Bathroom & Toilet Sanitation: Deep cleaning of tiles, fittings, and floors; disinfection of high-touch areas; removal of stains and residues
  • Vacuuming & Upholstery Care: Vacuum cleaning of carpets, rugs, and upholstery; spot cleaning and deodorizing
  • Window & Glass Cleaning: Interior glass surface cleaning; frame and sill dust removal
  • Waste Management: Segregation of dry and wet waste; timely disposal per local guidelines

Residential housekeeping enhances cleanliness and promotes a healthier living environment for family members and residents.
